How the challenge works.

  • 01 /

    Six waves of four days.

    Three working days, one rest day. The waves climb: 10-12-15, then 15-18-20, then 20-23-25, and so on until wave six hits 40-43-45. Final two days are a plateau check and a one-set test.

  • 02 /

    Break the day across your breaks.

    You do not need to do 30 pushups in one set. Four Pomodoro breaks a day means 7-8 pushups each break hits the target. That is the whole point: micro exercise, spread through the day.

  • 03 /

    Rest days are not optional.

    Every fourth day is rest. Walk, water, do nothing. Your muscles rebuild on rest days, not on push days. Skipping rest is how people flame out in week two.

  • 04 /

    Tick the box when done.

    Each cell has a small square. Fill it in. Visual proof the day moved. By day 30 the grid is a receipt of a month that was not wasted.

  • 05 /

    Day 30 is a test, not a target.

    One set. No breaks. As many as you have in you. If you do not hit 50, you did not fail. You now know your baseline, and the next 30 days start from there.

  • 06 /

    The math compounds.

    About 770 pushups total in 30 days. Run it four times a year and you have done 3,000 pushups you would otherwise have scrolled past. By October you are stronger than the version of you who read this in January.

The science behind it.

Sports scientists call short bouts of exercise spread through the day exercise snacks. A 2022 review by Islam, Gibala and Little in Exercise and Sport Sciences Reviews defines them as isolated bouts of vigorous exercise under one minute, repeated through the day. In a 2019 study by Jenkins et al. in Applied Physiology, Nutrition, and Metabolism, sedentary adults doing three short stair climbing bouts a day, three days a week, improved VO2 peak by about five percent in six weeks. The pattern interrupts the metabolic cost of prolonged sitting. The math behind this challenge uses the same principle: small, repeated, over many days.

Who this is for.

  • ·

    Desk workers sitting eight hours a day.

    The break was going to happen anyway. Now it builds something.

  • ·

    Beginners who have never done a pushup program.

    Starts at 10. Climbs gently. Rest days built in. No baseline test required to start.

  • ·

    People who quit gym memberships.

    No gym. No change of clothes. No commute. The floor is the equipment.

  • ·

    Teams, couples, roommates.

    Print one for each person. Hang them side by side. Peer pressure is free.

  • ·

    Anyone who likes crossing boxes off.

    Thirty boxes. One a day. Visible proof the month moved.

Print tips

  • A4, 210 by 297 mm. Fits US Letter if you shrink to fit on print dialog.
  • Print one and start today. Print thirty and give them to your office.
  • Want to track more than one round? Print a fresh sheet every 30 days. Keep the old ones as a stack.
  • Laminate it and use a dry-erase marker if you want to run the challenge again and again.
